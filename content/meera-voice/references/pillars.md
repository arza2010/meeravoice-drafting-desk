# Content pillars

Her published labels map to four pillars. Use the pillar names below when planning; each piece should sit in one primary pillar.

| Pillar | Her published categories | Published count | What it does for the brand |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Formulation Literacy | Ingredient Deep-Dive, Formulation Science, Consumer Education | 7 of 15 | Core authority. Teaches the reader to evaluate formulations, not ingredients. |
| 2. Industry Transparency | Industry Transparency | 3 of 15 | Positions against marketing language (clinically tested, natural, clean). Earns the "honest one in the room" position. |
| 3. Founder Proof | Founder Story, Brand Philosophy | 4 of 15 | Origin (pH 3.2 meeting), principles (no fragrance, not a sales list), honest retros. Converts authority into trust in Skinstinct. |
| 4. Built for India | India-Specific Context | 1 of 15 | Climate-validated formulation, humidity, Indian regulation, Indian UV. **Her most differentiated and least-used pillar.** |

## Recurring IP (ownable ideas — reuse and name them consistently)
- **The label-to-formulation gap** — the umbrella idea.
- **The three conditions for an active**: reaches the site of action; at a concentration sufficient to trigger the response; intact and bioavailable at contact.
- **The three things a percentage doesn't tell you**: pH, delivery base, batch consistency.
- **The ask-for-documentation test**: ask in writing for pH, CoA, stability data, study design; a vague or missing answer is useful information.
- **Climate validation**: ask what climate a base was validated for; demand stability testing under Indian temperature and humidity.
- **Documented, not claimed**: Skinstinct practices described as documentation (mid-batch CoA sampling, 3-month stability intervals in year one, pH on every batch).

## Recommended mix (going forward)
Roughly: Formulation Literacy 35%, Built for India 25%, Industry Transparency 20%, Founder Proof 20%. The India pillar is where she has data no imported brand can match; it deserves more airtime, especially on LinkedIn where founders, manufacturers, and investors are reading.

## Angle bank (starting points, verify all facts with Meera)
Formulation Literacy: retinol vehicles (oil vs emulsion); how to read an INCI order (the 1% line); AHA pH and why "10% glycolic" can be inert; what a CoA actually contains; accelerated vs real-time stability testing.
Built for India: monsoon vs winter routines by base, not by active; hard water and cleansers; sunscreen texture and reapplication in humidity; storage in Indian bathrooms (heat, steam); Indian regulatory status of common claims.
Industry Transparency: "dermatologist approved"; "non-comedogenic"; "hypoallergenic"; percentage-led branding; influencer ingredient trends vs evidence.
Founder Proof: what the contract manufacturer pushed back on; what the stability protocol costs; the Vitamin C product's unsolved problems (only what she's willing to share); what customer questions taught her.
